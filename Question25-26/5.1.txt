
5.(i).Explain the significance of micro-operations in the execution of machine-level instructions. How do micro - operations like arithmetic, logic, and shift operations contribute to the processing of instructions in a digital computer?


=> Significance of Micro-operations in Instruction Execution:

Definition: A micro-operation is a basic operation performed on data stored in registers during a clock pulse. A machine-level instruction is executed through a sequence of micro-operations.
Significance of Micro-operations:

1. Instruction execution: They break complex machine instructions into simple steps.
2. Data processing: They help perform arithmetic, logical, and shift operations on data.
3. Register transfer: They move data between registers and other computer components.
4. Control of hardware: They help the control unit generate signals to operate the CPU.
5. Efficient execution: They ensure instructions are executed in a proper sequence.

=>Types of Micro-operations:

1. Arithmetic Micro-operations:
These perform mathematical operations on register data, such as addition, subtraction, increment, and decrement.
Example: R3 <- R1 + R2
It adds the contents of registers R1 and R2 and stores the result in R3.

2. Logic Micro-operations:
These perform bitwise logical operations such as AND, OR, XOR, and NOT.
Example: R3 <- R1 AND R2
It performs an AND operation on the corresponding bits of R1 and R2.

3. Shift Micro-operations:
These shift the bits of a register to the left or right. They include logical, arithmetic, and circular shifts.
Example: R1 <- shl R1
It shifts the bits of R1 one position to the left.

=>Role of Micro-operations in Instruction Execution:

Consider the instruction:
ADD R1, R2

The CPU performs the following basic steps:
1. Fetch the instruction from memory.
2. Decode the instruction using the control unit.
3. Read the contents of registers R1 and R2.
4. Perform the addition using the ALU.
5. Store the result in the destination register.
These steps involve data transfer, arithmetic processing, and control signals.

Conclusion:
Micro-operations are essential for executing machine-level instructions. Arithmetic operations perform calculations, logic operations perform bitwise comparisons and manipulations, and shift operations move bits. Together, they enable the CPU to process data and execute instructions correctly.
