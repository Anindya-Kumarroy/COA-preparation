
6.(i).a.Describe floating-point representation in computers.



=>FLOATING-POINT REPRESENTATION:

Definition:
Floating-point representation is a method of representing real numbers,
including very large and very small numbers, in a computer.

Main Parts:
1. Sign bit (S): 0 means positive, 1 means negative.
2. Exponent (E): Biased exponent, it determines the position of the binary point.
3. Mantissa (Significand): Stores the significant digits of the number.

Formula:
x = (-1)^s * (1.f)2 * 2^(E - bias)

Normalisation:
The number is stored in the form 1.f. The leading 1 is not stored
(hidden bit).

IEEE 754 Standard:

                 Sign    Exponent    Fraction    Bias
Single (32-bit)  1 bit   8 bits      23 bits     127
Double (64-bit)  1 bit   11 bits     52 bits     1023

Example: Represent -13.25 in 32-bit format

Step 1: Sign = 1 (negative number)
Step 2: 13.25 = 1101.01 (binary)
Step 3: Normalise: 1101.01 = 1.10101 * 2^3
Step 4: Exponent: E = 3 + 127 = 130 = 10000010 (binary)
Step 5: Fraction = 10101 followed by zeros, to make 23 bits
        = 10101000000000000000000

Result (32-bit):
Sign | Exponent   | Fraction
1    | 10000010   | 10101000000000000000000

Special Values:
- Exponent all 0s: zero or de-normal numbers
- Exponent all 1s: infinity or NaN

Advantages:
- Provides a wide range of values.
- Useful for scientific calculations, graphics and engineering applications.

Disadvantage:
- Rounding errors occur (for example, 0.1 cannot be stored exactly).





b. Represent the number 24.25 in the IEEE 754 in both formats.


=>24.25 in IEEE 754:

Step 1: Convert to binary:
Whole part: 24 = 11000
Fraction part: 0.25 = .01 (because 0.25 = 1/4)
So 24.25 = 11000.01

Step 2: Normalise:
Move the point so only one 1 is before it:
11000.01 = 1.100001 × 2⁴
Sign = positive, so sign bit = 0
Exponent = 4

step 3:
Mantissa (the part after the point) = 100001

Single precision (32-bit):
Format: 1 sign bit, 8 exponent bits, 23 mantissa bits. Bias = 127.
Stored exponent = 4 + 127 = 131 = 10000011
Mantissa = 100001 followed by zeros to fill 23 bits: 10000100000000000000000

Result:
sign   | Exponent | Fraction
0      | 10000011 | 10000100000000000000000

Hex: 0x41C20000

Double precision (64-bit)

Format: 1 sign bit, 11 exponent bits, 52 mantissa bits. Bias = 1023.

Stored exponent = 4 + 1023 = 1027 = 10000000011
Mantissa = 100001 followed by zeros to fill 52 bits: 1000010000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000

Result:

sign | Exponent    | Fraction
0    | 10000000011 | 1000010000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000

Hex: 0x4038400000000000
